About your photos

Imported photos are saved locally in this browser. Google Drive pictures are fetched privately after you sign in; they are not uploaded to GitHub. This is not a cloud-synced catalogue database: new Drive photos appear when you tap Refresh, and you choose which to import. Browser storage can be lost if you clear site data.
